# App icon

The app icon is an original abstract gauge with teal and orange arcs on a dark background.

The asset is located at `App/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/AppIcon.png`:

- 1024 × 1024 pixels.
- Opaque RGB PNG.
- Square artwork; iOS supplies the outer icon mask.
- The AppIcon asset is selected for Debug and Release builds.

After changing the artwork, rebuild and install the app to update the Home Screen icon.
